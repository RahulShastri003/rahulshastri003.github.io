# Miscellany

A small collection of problems, books, and other things I have come across and found interesting enough to keep a note of. Some problems have solutions, others may not, and their original sources are not always known. There is no particular theme or order.

## Interesting Problems

**1. Three Circles and Seven Tokens.** Seven white tokens occupy the seven regions of three overlapping circles. In one move, we may either flip all four tokens inside a chosen circle (white to black and black to white), or make all tokens in that circle white. Can we reach a configuration in which only the central token, common to all three circles, is black? [Problem and solution (PDF)](/documents/problems/three-circles-seven-tokens.pdf)
